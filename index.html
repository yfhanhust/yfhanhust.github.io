About

About me

I am a Research Scientist in the PIRAT project team at Inria Rennes. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity: understanding how learning systems fail under adversarial conditions, establishing stronger security and privacy guarantees, and designing AI methods that remain useful in realistic security environments.

Before joining Inria, I was a Senior Principal Researcher at Symantec Research Labs, where I worked on AI-driven cyberattack classification and prediction, spear-phishing analysis, malware intelligence, and adversarial robustness. I received my PhD from the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
