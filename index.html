Research

Trustworthy AI, under real-world constraints

My work connects the foundations of robust machine learning with security systems that operate on incomplete, noisy, and adversarial data.

01

Security of AI systems

Characterizing attacks against deep neural networks, graph models, federated learning, and large language models—and developing principled mitigations.

  • Backdoors
  • Privacy attacks
  • Adversarial robustness
02

AI for cybersecurity

Learning-based methods for malware classification, intrusion detection, source-code vulnerability analysis, and security-event prediction.

  • Malware
  • NIDS
  • Vulnerability detection
03

Knowledge-grounded reasoning

Combining knowledge graphs and language models to encode, interpret, and reason about cyber incidents with transparent evidence.

  • Knowledge graphs
  • LLMs
  • Threat intelligence
